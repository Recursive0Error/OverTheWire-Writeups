## Writeup Format

Each challenge writeup follows this structure:

1. **Challenge** - Name, category, difficulty, and challenge URL or event.
2. **Problem statement** - A short summary of what the challenge provides and asks for.
3. **Initial observations** - Files, services, clues, output, or other facts noticed first.
4. **Approach** - The hypotheses and steps tried, including useful commands and their relevant output.
5. **Breakthrough** - The observation or mistake that revealed the correct direction.
6. **Solution** - The final reproducible steps, with commands explained briefly.
7. **Flag** - The captured flag, when appropriate.
8. **Takeaways** - The technique, concept, or lesson learned.

## New Challenge Notes

For each challenge, send me:

- The challenge name, category, and difficulty.
- The prompt and any supplied files or connection details.
- What you noticed and tried, including failed attempts that helped.
- The commands, tools, outputs, and reasoning that led to the solution.
- The final flag and any details you want kept private.

I will turn that account into a numbered Markdown writeup, add it to the table above, and keep the format consistent across the repository.
